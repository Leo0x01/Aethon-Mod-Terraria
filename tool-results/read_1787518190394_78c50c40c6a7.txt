     8→## Project Vision
     9→
    10→The user dreamt of a Terraria mod centered on an ancient cosmic entity that is the final boss.
    11→The player finds a small altar in the world next to a glowing "light" item that can transform
    12→into one of several weapons (bow, sword, kinetic-energy cannon, mage book). The weapon is a
    13→fragment of the entity's power, levels infinitely by killing enemies, grants escalating skill
    14→points, and unlocks a procedurally-generated skill tree unique per weapon — eventually letting
    15→the weapon absorb abilities from every weapon of the same class in the game.
    16→
    17→## Expanded & Improved Concept (Design Document)
    18→
    19→### 1. The Cosmic Entity — "Aethon, the Primordial Light"
    20→- An ageless being that predates Terraria's universe. Its body IS a galaxy; its thoughts are
    21→  gravitational tides. Long ago it fractured itself to seed all creation — every star, every
    22→  soul, is a splinter of Aethon.
    23→- A fragment of its true consciousness fell asleep inside Terraria's world, buried in stone.
    24→- Civilizations rose and fell worshipping it; only ruined altars remain.
    25→- The final boss: when the player's shard-weapon reaches a resonance threshold, Aethon stirs.
    26→  Defeating it doesn't destroy it — it *acknowledges* the player as a peer consciousness.
    27→
    28→### 2. The Discovery — The Altar & the Light
    29→- Found naturally in a new sub-biome: **The Hollow Sanctum** (a crystalline cavern biome that
    30→  can generate anywhere underground after the player has 200 max HP).
    31→- The altar offers a single floating mote of pure light: **the Genesis Shard**.
    32→- The shard lets the player CHOOSE its form (Bow / Sword / Cannon / Book). Choice is permanent
    33→  for that character (a "soul-binding" ritual). Each form unlocks a wholly separate progression.
    34→- The shard visually evolves with level: dim mote → glowing orb → runed artifact → star-forged
    35→  relic → miniature galaxy in the player's hands.
    36→
    37→### 3. The Four Weapons (Genesis Forms)
    38→
    39→| Form | Name | Class | Fantasy |
    40→|------|------|-------|---------|
    41→| Bow | **Lumina, the Starbowed** | Ranged | Arrows woven from starlight, seek their mark |
    42→| Sword | **Solbrand, Edge of Dawn** | Melee | A blade of condensed dawn, cuts reality |
    43→| Cannon | **Voidcannon / Pulse Driver** | Ranged (bullets) | Kinetic-energy cannon firing plasma slugs |
    44→| Book | **Grimoire of the Eternal** | Magic | Spells sourced from the entity's memory |
    45→
    46→### 4. Infinite Leveling System (XP from kills)
    47→- Every enemy killed grants XP based on its tier (slime: 1, zombie: 3, boss: 5000+).
    48→- XP curve: `xpForLevel(n) = 80 * n^1.5` (smooth exponential). Levels are uncapped — truly infinite.
    49→
    50→**Skill-point reward schedule (clarified & extended):**
    51→
    52→| Level range | Points / level | Cumulative points at top of range |
    53→|-------------|----------------|-----------------------------------|
    54→| 1 – 10      | 1              | 10                                |
    55→| 11 – 20     | 2              | 30                                |
    56→| 21 – 30     | 3              | 60                                |
    57→| 31 – 40     | 4              | 100                               |
    58→| 41 – 50     | 5              | 150                               |
    59→| 51 – 60     | 6              | 210                               |
    60→| 61 – 70     | 7              | 280                               |
    61→| 71 – 80     | 8              | 360                               |
    62→| 81 – 90     | 9              | 450                               |
    63→| 91 – 100    | 10             | 550                               |
    64→| 101+        | 10 (flat)      | +10 each level, forever           |
    65→
    66→### 5. Procedural Skill Trees (per weapon)
    67→Each weapon generates its OWN skill tree using a seeded procedural algorithm. The tree:
    68→- Is a constellation-style node graph (cosmic visual theme, nodes = stars, edges = light-bridges).
    69→- Has 6 branches per weapon. Branches are themed; node layout & rarity rolls are randomized
    70→  per character seed → replayability.
    71→- Three node rarities: Common (solid), Rare (glowing), Legendary (pulsing supernova).
    72→- Some nodes are gated by total points invested or weapon level.
    73→
    74→**Branches by weapon (extended & deepened):**
    75→
    76→#### Grimoire of the Eternal (Magic)
    77→1. **Mana Flow** — max mana, regen, reduced cast cost, "mana well" passive node.
    78→2. **Elemental Genesis** — fire / frost / storm / void elements; projectiles change element.
    79→3. **Projectile Evolution** — split, homing, chain, orbital familiars, multi-cast.
    80→4. **Arcane Conversion** — mana→HP, HP→mana, damage as % of missing mana, lifesteal.
    81→5. **Cosmic Spells** — Black Hole pull, Supernova burst, Time Dilation slow, Starfall rain.
    82→6. **Lore Absorption** ⭐ — the fantasy capstone: absorb projectiles/stats/effects of EVERY
    83→   magic weapon in the base game (and loaded mods). Pick which to equip as "Memory Runes".
    84→
    85→#### Lumina, the Starbowed (Ranged — bow)
    86→1. **Arrow Genesis** — light, void, piercing, homing, splitting arrow types.
    87→2. **Quiver Mastery** — extra arrows per shot, faster draw, no-ammo nodes.
    88→3. **Hunter's Mark** — tagging enemies, crit stacking, weak-spot detection.
    89→4. **Celestial Shots** — Meteor Volley, Solar Flare shot, Eclipse (blind + burn AoE).
    90→5. **Phantom Quiver** — ethereal arrows that pass terrain, ricochet, Bouncing Betty.
    91→6. **Lore Absorption** ⭐ — absorb every bow weapon's signature arrow/behavior.
    92→
    93→#### Solbrand, Edge of Dawn (Melee)
    94→1. **Blade Genesis** — slash wave, beam slash, whirl, thrust combo types.
    95→2. **Combo Mastery** — escalating combo meter, finisher moves, parry-riposte chain.
    96→3. **Solar Wrath** — Sunflare slash (burn + blind), Eclipse Cleave, Corona aura.
    97→4. **Aegis of Dawn** — parry frames, damage reflection, invuln dashes.
    98→5. **Weight of Stars** — heavy hits with knockback, ground-slam shockwaves.
    99→6. **Lore Absorption** ⭐ — absorb every sword's signature swing/special.
   100→
   101→#### Voidcannon / Pulse Driver (Ranged — bullets)
   102→1. **Slug Genesis** — kinetic, plasma, void, emp, cryo ammo types.
   103→2. **Rapid Fire** — fire rate, mag size, reload speed, dual-trigger.
   104→3. **Precision** — accuracy, headshots, crit multiplier, scope zoom.
   105→4. **Heavy Ordnance** — charged shots, artillery mode, beam-overcharge.
   106→5. **Recoil Engineering** — recoil dash, rocket-jump, knockback immunity.
   107→6. **Lore Absorption** ⭐ — absorb every gun's signature bullet/behavior.
   108→
   109→### 6. The "Lore Absorption" Capstone (deepened)
   110→This is the heart of the user's vision. Each weapon's final branch lets it LEARN abilities
   111→from every other weapon of the same class in the game:
   112→- A "Memory Codex" UI lists all base-game (and modded) weapons of the class.
   113→- The player invests absorption points (earned via a second currency: **Resonance Shards**
   114→  dropped by bosses & cosmic events) to "memorize" a weapon's signature behavior.
   115→- Memorized behaviors are slotted as "Runes" — the player can equip up to N at once (N grows
   116→  with weapon level). This gives near-infinite build variety.
   117→- Example: the Grimoire can memorize the Magic Dagger's throw, the Demon Scythe's boomerang,
   118→  the Lunar Flare's rain, the Last Prism's converging beam, ALL at once.
   119→
   120→### 7. World Systems around the Weapon
   121→- **Cosmic Events** triggered by weapon level thresholds:
   122→  - Lv 25: **Starlight Rain** (ambient meteor shower, rare ore spawns).
   123→  - Lv 50: **The Hollowing** (Hollow Sanctum spreads, new mobs).
   124→  - Lv 75: **Dimensional Rifts** (mini-dungeons with loot).
   125→  - Lv 100: **Aethon's Stirring** (mini-boss Echoes of past wielders spawn).
   126→  - Lv 150+: **The Awakening** (final boss becomes available).
   127→- **Resonance** — weapon gains zone bonuses (e.g., +20% XP in Space, +damage in Underworld).
   128→- **Echoes** — shadow-clones of previous shard-wielders, optional difficulty bosses with lore.
   129→- **The Witness** — a wandering cosmic NPC who narrates lore as you level, sells Memory Runes,
   130→  and gives the final hint to face Aethon.
   131→
   132→### 8. Final Boss — Aethon, the Primordial Light
   133→- Five-phase fight across collapsing cosmic arena.
   134→- Phase themes: Stardust → Nebula → Gravity → Black Hole → Acknowledgment.
   135→- Mechanics: reality bends (gravity flips, time slows), the player's own absorbed abilities
   136→  are reflected back at them (Aethon wields YOUR build against you).
   137→- Defeat = Aethon "acknowledges" you; the Genesis Shard fully awakens, unlocking cosmetic
   138→  "Ascended" forms and a New Game+ cosmic difficulty.
   139→
   140→### 9. Quality-of-life & meta
   141→- **Boss Checklist** integration, **Recipe Browser** compatibility.
   142→- Config options: disable infinite leveling cap (set max), tune XP rate, disable cosmic events.
   143→- Multiplayer-friendly: each player has their own shard; party-wide resonance bonuses.

   835→## QA Findings
   836→- **Bug found & fixed: codex persist effect racing with skill-tree persist.** When a build was
   837→  loaded (via preset or import), both the skill-tree and codex persist effects wrote to the URL
   838→  hash. The codex effect sometimes ran first with stale state, clobbering the skill-tree nodes
   839→  from the hash (resulting in `v1.m.115.` with empty node segment even though the skill tree
   840→  had 15 nodes in state). Fixed by adding a `lastCodexSig` ref to the codex persist effect that
   841→  tracks the last-written codex signature (`cls|memorized|level`); the effect now skips writing
   842→  if the codex didn't change, eliminating the race. Verified: loading Arcane Storm now correctly
   843→  produces hash `v1.m.115.0,1,2,3,4,a,b,c,d,e,k,l,m,n,o` (15 nodes preserved).
