The '''Reality Slasher''' is a craftable post-[[Moon Lord]] [[radiant weapon]]. When swung, it will produce a double-edged scythe that spins in a full circle around the player, damaging [[enemies]] and inflicting the [[Light Curse]] [[debuff]]. With each use of the scythe, the nearest unobstructed enemy will be slashed either horizontally or vertically by straight purple "cuts" 3 times, dealing additional damage.

Upon directly striking an enemy, the user will gain 3 stacks of [[Soul Essence]]. The purple cuts will not grant any soul essence.
