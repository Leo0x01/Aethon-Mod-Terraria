The '''Exoblade''' is a craftable post-[[Moon Lord]] [[melee weapon]].

When used with its primary fire ({{key|use}}), it uses a custom overhead swing animation that fires 4 bright rainbow beams which home in on enemies after traveling for 0.4 seconds. On enemy hits, each beam creates a long, infinitely piercing slash that can hit multiple times. The beams and their slashes deal 35% of the weapon's damage.

When used with its secondary fire ({{key|open}}), the player holds the sword and dashes in the direction of the cursor for 0.82 seconds, with a base distance of 65 tiles. Upon contact with an enemy, the player bounces back and 5 beam slashes appear on the target, each dealing 150% of the weapon's damage. After hitting an enemy with the dash attack, the next swing attack within 0.62 seconds will be 50% larger and create a massive explosion which deals 180% of the weapon's damage. The dash has a 1-second cooldown. 

All of its attacks inflict the [[Miracle Blight]] [[debuff]], the dash attack also inflicts the [[Frozen]] debuff.
