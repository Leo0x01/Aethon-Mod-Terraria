The '''Reality Rupture''' is a craftable post-[[Moon Lord]] non-consumable [[javelin]] which is a direct upgrade to the [[Spear of Destiny]]. It alternates between throwing 3 small homing spears which pierce twice, and 1 piercing lance which deals 400% base damage, but loses 5% damage on each hit.

Performing a [[stealth strike]] with the Reality Rupture will throw a single fast reality-fracturing lance which explodes on hit, dealing 390% of the weapon's damage.
