Overview
Section titled “Overview”
Light of the Star Tomb is the magic branch of the neutron weapon line. Left click hurls a pulsar at the cursor. Hold right click to magnetically brake the ones already out, then release to crack them open. The stars are the sustain; the starquake is the harvest.
“The psychic meant to crush stars into black holes, but only ever made remnants frozen in degenerate matter. Nomads call that stretch of sky the Star Tomb.”

Combat Behavior
Section titled “Combat Behavior”
Left Click
Section titled “Left Click”
Throws a pulsar toward the cursor. It brakes on its own, settles near the aim point, then spins up. Once it anchors, two magnetic beams sweep with the spin. Non-boss fodder gets a weak pull toward the star.
Three may stand at once. Seeding a fourth forces the oldest into a weaker starquake, so the cast still counts. Hits apply
20 s
of Void Erosion and carry
80
armor penetration. A star lasts about
11 s
. Left click spends mana.
Right Click
Section titled “Right Click”
Charge only builds if a star on the field can still be braked. Holding packs spin into the crust; a full charge takes about
1 s
. Releasing triggers a starquake: the crust splits, a shock ring expands, and hostile projectiles inside it die. A fuller charge makes a larger, harder quake.
Quake hits apply
30 s
of Void Erosion and
100
armor penetration. Stars that survive the quake overclock for a while. Right click itself costs no mana.
