'''Dragonfire''' is a [[Debuffs#Debuff Resistances and Vulnerabilities|Heat]]-type [[debuff]] inflicted by [[Yharon, Dragon of Rebirth]], [[The Torch God]] in [[Death Mode]], [[Lava Chicken Broth]] and equipment dropped by Yharon. It causes the player to lose 25 health per second, disables natural life regeneration and causes [[enemies]] to lose 480 health per second.

If the target is inflicted with [[Oiled]], they will lose 505 health per second instead of 480.

== Resistances ==
The player can become immune to Dragonfire by equipping the following items:
{{item immunities|1}}

*If the player has the [[Dynamo Stem Cells]] [[accessory]] equipped, they lose 12.5 health per second instead of 25.
