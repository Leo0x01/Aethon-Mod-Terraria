Brimstone Witch Map icon Statistics Resistances Sounds Hurt Killed Brimstone Hellblast Enchant Exhume The Brimstone Witch is a post- Moon Lord NPC vendor that will spawn once the following conditions have been met: There is an empty house. Supreme Witch, Calamitas has been defeated. When threatened by enemies , she will defend herself with Brimstone Hellblasts. Contents 1 Enchant 2 Living preferences 3 Names 4 Quotes 4.1 Interaction Quotes 4.2 Happiness Quotes 5 Notes 6 Trivia 7 History Enchant For a full explanation on this mechanic, see Enchantment . For an expensive price, the Brimstone Witch is capable of enchanting the player's items, granting them new power with some drawback. She is also capable of "exhuming" certain items, transforming them into entirely new items. Living preferences Names The Brimstone Witch will always be named Calamitas. Quotes Interaction Quotes Happiness Quotes Notes All dialogue has a 1/4444 chance to be replaced with "Mrrp is cringe." In the get fixed boi seed, her hellblasts inflict the Vulnerability Hex debuff . Trivia The Bestiary entry for the Brimstone Witch: "Recruited to Yharim's forces late into the war, her magic overflows with such power that she is largely incapable of casting normal spells. Yet despite her great power, Calamitas prefers not to use it, as it takes a heavy toll on her psyche." Her HP is the same amount as her boss form in Normal Mode. When clicking Donors button, the Brimstone Witch says the names of several people who have donated a certain amount to the Calamity Mod on Patreon, randomly selected.
