 FAQ
 Frequently Asked Questions
 What kind of simulations are on mysimulator.uk?▾
 26,000+ interactive browser simulations across 552 categories: physics, space, chaos theory, biology, machine learning, quantum physics and fluid dynamics. All run in real time — no installation required.
 Do the simulations require any installation or login?▾
 No. Everything runs entirely in your browser — nothing to install, no account, no plugins. Works on Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Safari; many also work on mobile.
 Are these simulations scientifically accurate?▾
 They use the same mathematical formulations as professional software: Navier-Stokes, Kepler-Leapfrog, Hodgkin-Huxley, Lennard-Jones. Designed for learning and intuition-building.
 Can I use these simulations for teaching?▾
 Yes — all simulations are free and designed for educational use: university lectures, high-school physics classes and self-directed STEM learning. Link directly to any simulation or embed it via an iframe.
 Can I embed a simulation in my own website or LMS?▾
 Yes. Every simulation is a self-contained page — embed it with an iframe. Recommended size is 1024×768 or larger. No API key or attribution required for educational use.
 How often are new simulations added?▾
 Approximately 3 new simulations per week, grouped into themed weekly waves. Each release includes a devlog post explaining the algorithms and design decisions.
