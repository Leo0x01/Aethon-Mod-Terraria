The '''Subsuming Vortex''' is a craftable post-[[Moon Lord]] [[spell tome]].

When used with its primary fire ({{key|use}}), it rapidly fires a spread of 3 homing vortices, each dealing 30% of the weapon's damage and piercing infinitely.

When used with its secondary fire ({{key|open}}), it charges up a large vortex that appears on the spell tome, dealing damage on hit. The vortex periodically consumes mana to fire smaller, faster vortices. After about 1 second of charging, it can be launched towards the cursor by releasing the fire button. After 4 seconds, the vortex becomes fully charged and stops firing small vortices. The launched large vortex causes a massive explosion on hit, which rapidly deals damage. The damage of the explosion scales with how long the vortex has been charged, up to 465% of the weapon's damage.

All of its attacks inflict the [[Miracle Blight]] [[debuff]].
