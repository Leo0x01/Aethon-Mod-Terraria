The '''Terragrim''' is a [[pre-Hardmode]] [[melee weapon]]. When used, it rapidly releases slashes aimed towards the cursor. Anything within this area takes constant damage at a rate exceeding the weapon's stated [[use time]].

{{float file box|width=188px|title=Sword in Stone
Background Objects|[[File:Enchanted Sword comparison.png]]|A junk (left) and enchanted (right) sword in stone. Only the right one has a chance to drop the Terragrim.}}

It is obtainable from the unique [[background object]] found in an [[Enchanted Sword Shrine]] or very rarely in the Caverns layer. When the object is destroyed, it has a {{chance|1/30}} chance to drop a Terragrim rather than an [[Enchanted Sword]].

{{modifier|meleealt}} The Terragrim's unique means of attack is unaffected by any size and speed modifiers.
[[File:Terragrim (demo).gif|frame|right|The Terragrim attacking [[Target Dummy|Target Dummies]].]]

== Notes ==
* The Terragrim grants 5 [[global invincibility frame]]s on hit,{{source code ref|v=1.4.4.9|method=Terraria.Projectile.Damage}} meaning that it can hit the target 12 times per second.
* In [[Celebration Mk 10]] and [[Zenith (seed)|Zenith]] worlds, the Terragrim can be purchased from the [[Princess]] during a [[Blood Moon]] for {{iteminfo|Terragrim|buy}}.
* Although visually identical, Enchanted Swords placed by the [[Rubblemaker]] will never drop Terragrim when broken.{{source code ref|v=1.4.4.9|method=Terraria.WorldGen.Check3x2}}
* The Terragrim can hit behind the player at a very short distance.

== Tips ==
* Its high attack speed makes it very easy to defeat the [[Brain of Cthulhu]]'s second phase, allowing the user to stand still and avoid taking damage.
* The best [[world size]] for finding the Terragrim is medium. Although there are usually more Enchanted Sword Shrines in large worlds (2–4 generation attempts, as opposed to 1–3 in medium worlds), the distances between them are smaller in medium worlds, on average.
* Since the presence of the Terragrim is determined when the background object is destroyed, it is possible to force its appearance by "save scumming": Having found an Enchanted Sword Shrine with an enchanted sword in a stone, switch [[Autosave On|autosave]] off, and then keep breaking the background object and force-quitting ''[[Terraria]]'' (''not'' pressing "{{gameText|LegacyInterface.35}}" in the pause menu, but closing the ''Terraria'' application itself, e.g. via {{key|Alt}}+{{key|F4}} on {{icon/windows}} Windows) until obtaining a Terragrim instead of an Enchanted Sword.

== Trivia ==
* The Terragrim's name is a reference to the sword [https://castlevania.fandom.com/wiki/Crissaegrim Crissaegrim], also known as the Valmanway, from the 1997 action role-playing platformer game ''{{wikipedia|Castlevania: Symphony of the Night}}''. Like the [[Arkhalis]], the Terragrim's function and appearance are also based on the Crissaegrim.
* Although the Terragrim can appear in the [[Zenith]]'s attack animation, it is not required as a crafting material to create it.
* Even if using an [[inventory editor]] to obtain the Legendary modifier, it will not change how fast the sword slashes or how large the slashes are.
* The Terragrim is one of the very few weapons that can be acquired as physical objects in the game's world, the others being the [[Enchanted Sword]], the [[Water Bolt]], and [[Abigail's Flower]].
* The [[Arkhalis]] can be considered its upgrade.
* The Terragrim replaced the Arkhalis as a special weapon found in Enchanted Sword Shrines, as the Arkhalis is found in [[Arkhalis's set|Arkhalis's developer set]] as of [[1.4.0.1]].
* The Terragrim emits light when used and releases light-emitting particles. The particles travel towards the direction the player is aiming, although they do not tend to travel very far when aiming down or up.
